## Correctness decisions

- There is no universal gambling win rate or universal individual negative EV.
  Labels distinguish model probabilities, pot share, house edge and cash return.
- The 21-point long-run examples use infinite-deck DAS/no-surrender assumptions,
  separately from the single-hand adviser that permits surrender.
- The wage basis is MOM's S$12.04 gross hourly PWM rate for eligible counter
  attendants, 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30. It is not McDonald's official pay.
- Time is observable visible-page time, tracked separately per tool. It cannot
  measure play outside the page. Net-return comparisons do not value learning
  or entertainment, nor represent actual wages lost.
- Interrupted fault injection was reproduced: the two-player conditional
  probability used the wrong model. The original backup matched Git; it was
  restored. Future mutations use disposable copies only.
